Welcome to ArchIdle

Welcome to ArchIdle, a pet project for an idle game about gathering resources, recruiting workers and expanding your territory.

This is intended to be a spiritual successor to my other idle game project, allowing it to expand onto more features rather than being restricted to a single theme. I do, however, intend to incorporate its ideas here in some way or another.

At the present moment there isn't really much of a game yet, moreso testing several things, but you're still free to check it out over here.
